## Description (paste as is)

It is 4 o'clock in the morning and 2 of 3 packs from Line 3 leak in the leak tester. About 6,000 packs go on hold. The general manager will ask two questions: why does this keep happening, and what is it costing us?

This walkthrough answers both, from a blank page, in RCA Pilot. One example, step by step:
- the problem, drafted from rough shift notes (the AI turns the notes into a clear statement and lists what is missing)
- the loss in dollars, with cash cost and lost margin
- the evidence, before opinions
- the cause tree: physical, human and system causes, natural versus forced wear, 5 Whys and Fishbone views
- AI help that only suggests (Cause coach, Challenge my analysis, Suggested actions): you accept or dismiss every suggestion
- corrective and preventive actions, with an owner, a due date and a cost, and the maintenance plan changes
- verifying that the failure stopped, so savings only count once they are proven, then rolling the countermeasure out to similar assets
- the management summary and report

All figures in the example are illustrative, made up to show the method. They are not from a real site or customer.
