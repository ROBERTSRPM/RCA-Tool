VOICEOVER FOR WALKTHROUGH: READING VERSION (RCA Pilot)
1440 words, about 10 minutes at your normal easy pace

How to read it
- Record one clip per scene. Press record, say "Scene 1", wait 2 seconds, read, then stop. Next scene, new clip.
- Each line is one breath. Read the line, take a small breath, read the next line.
- A blank line means a longer pause, like a full stop in conversation.
- Lines ending in a comma carry on: keep your voice up and continue.
- If you stumble, pause, then say the whole line again. Don't stop the recording.
- Talk to one person, as if you're telling a colleague over coffee.

Now we get to Step 4, the Cause tree, where the real thinking happens.

Because the seals were weak along the flange.

Why?

Because the jaw ran 17 degrees low, with worn silicone,

If we stopped there we'd be back in a few weeks,

and nothing in the maintenance plan checks jaw temperature or replaces that silicone.

That's the system level and the real root,
so I mark it with This is a root cause.

The other branches work the same way:
wrong settings, because operators raised speed and cut dwell to keep up,
a new film batch,
and a seal check of only 3 packs every 2 hours.

For each cause I tick the evidence and mark it Verified or Ruled out,
because if I can't prove it, it stays a theory.
