Voiceover for Walkthrough

How to read it
- Record one clip per scene. Press record, say "Scene 1", wait 2 seconds, read, then stop. Next scene, new clip.
- Each line is one breath. Read the line, take a small breath, read the next line.
- A blank line means a longer pause, like a full stop in conversation.
- Lines ending in a comma carry on: keep your voice up and continue.
- If you stumble, pause, then say the whole line again. Don't stop the recording.
- Talk to one person, as if you're telling a colleague over coffee.
