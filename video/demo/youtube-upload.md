## Description (paste as is)

Same pump, same failure, another night of lost production. RCA Pilot takes a maintenance or plant team from breakdown to verified fix, step by step.

In this 3-minute demo I walk through a sample investigation on a slurry pump (P-101) with repeat bearing and mechanical seal failures:
- the loss worked out in dollars
- evidence linked to each cause
- a cause tree with physical, human and system causes, and natural versus forced deterioration
- corrective and preventive actions, maintenance plan changes and spares
- savings that only count once the failure is confirmed stopped, and rolling the fix out to similar assets
- a one-page management summary

The AI suggests and your people decide. The cause coach, "Challenge my analysis" and suggested actions only propose. Nothing goes in until you accept it, and nothing is marked verified because of a suggestion.

The sample figures are illustrative. They are not from a real site or customer.
