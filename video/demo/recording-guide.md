# Recording guide: RCA Pilot demo voiceover

About 10 minutes. You only need your phone. Script: `script.md` (8 short scenes, about 2 minutes in total).

## Set up
- Pick a quiet small room with soft furnishings (a bedroom or a wardrobe with clothes is ideal). Avoid kitchens, bathrooms and empty rooms: they echo.
- Switch the phone to flight mode or Do Not Disturb. Turn off fans, air conditioning and the fridge if you can.
- Use the Voice Memos (iPhone) or Recorder (Android) app. Record at the best quality setting.
- Hold or prop the phone 15 to 20 cm from your mouth, a little to one side or slightly below, so breath does not hit the microphone. Do not touch the phone while recording.
- Sit or stand relaxed. Speak at normal conversation pace, calm and practical, as if explaining to a colleague. Do not rush; we can trim, we cannot add.

## Recording
1. Press record. Wait 2 seconds of silence.
2. Say the scene number, for example "Scene four". Pause 1 second.
3. Read the scene words exactly as written (the "Say" lines). One take per scene.
4. If you stumble, stop, pause 2 seconds, and say that line again from the start of the sentence. Do not stop the recording; we cut the mistake.
5. Leave a 2-second pause after the scene, then go straight to the next scene number.
6. Say the web address as: "w w w dot r c a pilot dot com".
7. Record all eight scenes in one file if you can. Several files are fine too, as long as every scene has its number said before it.

## Send it back
- Listen to a few seconds of the first take before doing the rest, to check it sounds clear and not too quiet.
- Upload the file(s) in the Claude app chat (the attach button). Send the original files, not a compressed or trimmed copy.

## Tips
- Drink some water first. Smile slightly on the opening line; it lifts the voice.
- Say the figures as written ("two hundred and thirty-eight thousand dollars").
- Skip hype. If a line feels too promotional, say it plainly and tell us; we can change the words.
