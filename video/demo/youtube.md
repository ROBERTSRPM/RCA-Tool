## Description

Does the same pump, gearbox or conveyor keep failing? RCA Pilot takes your team through root cause analysis step by step: write down the problem, put a dollar value on the loss, collect the evidence, build the cause tree, assign actions, and check that the failure really stopped.

In this demo I use a sample investigation on a slurry pump, P-101, with repeat seal and bearing failures. You will see the loss worked out in dollars, evidence linked to causes, a cause tree with physical, human and system causes (natural versus forced deterioration), maintenance plan changes with inspection intervals and spares, and a one-page management summary.

The AI suggests, and your people decide. The cause coach, "Challenge my analysis" and suggested actions only propose; nothing goes into the investigation until you accept it, and nothing is marked verified because of a suggestion. Savings count only once the failure is confirmed stopped, and you can roll a proven fix out to similar assets.

The sample figures are illustrative, not from a real customer.

I made RCA Pilot as a former Maintenance Manager, Production Manager and Site Manager, for maintenance, reliability, production and plant managers.
