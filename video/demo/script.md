# RCA Pilot product demo: voiceover script

Voice only (the owner), calm and practical. About 2:00. Sample investigation: slurry pump P-101. All figures are illustrative, taken from the sample data in the app, not from a real customer.

Spelling: Australian/British. Say "R-C-A Pilot". Say the web address as "w w w dot r c a pilot dot com".

| # | Scene | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction | 0:00-0:14 |
| 2 | Problem and loss | 0:14-0:29 |
| 3 | Evidence | 0:29-0:40 |
| 4 | Cause tree and Suggestions | 0:40-1:05 |
| 5 | Actions | 1:05-1:25 |
| 6 | Verify | 1:25-1:40 |
| 7 | Report | 1:40-1:50 |
| 8 | Close | 1:50-2:00 |

Target durations below are authoritative (they include a short pause at the end of each scene). Total 2:00, with the title card inside scene 1.

---

## Scene 1: Introduction (14 s)

**Say:** "Every repair feels like progress, until the same pump fails again. RCA Pilot helps your team find the real cause, fix it, and prove it stays fixed. Here it is, on a slurry pump."

**On screen:** Title card on a plain light background: "RCA Pilot — root cause analysis for maintenance, operations and plant teams" with "by Intelbeyond". After 5 s, cut to the dashboard with the sample investigation P-101 in the list. Caption: "Sample data".

## Scene 2: Problem and loss (15 s)

**Say:** "Start with the problem. Pump P-101 has failed six times in twelve months, and each failure stops the line for fourteen hours. Add the downtime, margin and repair cost, and RCA Pilot shows the loss: about two hundred and thirty-eight thousand dollars a year. These figures are illustrative."

**On screen:** Open the investigation. Problem step: title, asset, failure count and duration highlighted. Scroll to the Loss step; the loss total (about $238,440 a year) gets a subtle highlight and zoom. Caption: "Problem" then "Loss in dollars".

## Scene 3: Evidence (11 s)

**Say:** "Next, the evidence. Add photos, trend data, inspections and records, so every cause can be checked against facts, not opinions."

**On screen:** Evidence step: the list of evidence items (flow trend, bearing wear track, oil samples, seal face inspection, rebuild records). Slow scroll, highlight one item. Caption: "Evidence first".

## Scene 4: Cause tree and Suggestions (25 s)

**Say:** "Then the cause tree. Physical, human and system causes, marked as natural or forced deterioration. Under Suggestions, the cause coach proposes causes you may have missed, and Challenge my analysis points out weak links. You read each one, then accept it or dismiss it. The AI suggests. You decide."

**On screen:** Cause tree step. Show the three levels (physical, human, system) and the natural / forced marker. Open the Suggestions panel: click "Cause coach", wait (cut in the edit), highlight a suggestion and click Accept; dismiss another. Then click "Challenge my analysis" and show the result. Captions: "Physical, human, system", "Natural vs forced", "Suggestions: you accept or dismiss".

## Scene 5: Actions (20 s)

**Say:** "Now actions. Each root cause gets a corrective or preventive action, with an owner and a due date. Change the maintenance plan, with the new inspection interval, and list the spares. AI can suggest actions too, but nothing is added until you accept it."

**On screen:** Actions step. Show an action with owner and due date. Show a maintenance plan change with the interval (the seal flush check every week; the sample also has a monthly vibration reading) and a spares item. Click the AI "Suggested actions", highlight one, accept it. Captions: "Corrective and preventive actions", "Maintenance plan and spares".

## Scene 6: Verify (15 s)

**Say:** "After the fix, verify. Savings only count once someone has checked that the failure has stopped coming back. Then roll the fix out to similar assets, so the next pump does not fail the same way."

**On screen:** Verify step: the review date, "has it recurred" answer and the verified savings figure; the note that a saving counts only once actions are done and the review confirms no recurrence. Then the "similar assets" list for the roll-out. Captions: "Savings count once verified", "Roll out to similar assets".

## Scene 7: Report (10 s)

**Say:** "When you are ready, let the AI draft a one-page management summary. You edit it, then print or save it as a PDF."

**On screen:** Report step: click "Management summary (1 page)" under Write-up drafting, show the drafted summary (problem, loss, causes, actions, how we will know), then the Print / Save as PDF button. Caption: "One-page management summary".

## Scene 8: Close (10 s)

**Say:** "RCA Pilot keeps the whole investigation in one place. The AI suggests, and your people decide. Try it at w w w dot r c a pilot dot com."

**On screen:** Back to the cause tree with an accepted suggestion, fade to the end card: "RCA Pilot by Intelbeyond", "Try it at www.rcapilot.com".
