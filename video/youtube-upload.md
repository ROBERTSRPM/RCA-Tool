## Title
Root Cause Analysis Explained: How to Stop Fixing the Same Breakdown

## Description
Do you keep fixing the same pump, gearbox or conveyor? Every repair feels like progress, but it is a bill you keep paying. Root cause analysis (RCA) is how you find out why it really failed, fix it, and prove it stays fixed.

In this video:
- What RCA is, and what it is not
- Why it matters: uninterrupted operation, lower cost per unit, higher profit, and capacity without large CAPEX ("the hidden plant")
- The six parts of an effective RCA
- A full worked example: a slurry pump with repeat bearing and mechanical seal failures, from the loss in dollars to zero breakdowns in twelve months, and the difference between natural and forced deterioration

The numbers in the worked example are illustrative, to show the method. They are not benchmarks.

I have worked as a Maintenance Manager, Production Manager and Site/Plant Manager. This is for maintenance, reliability, production and plant managers in manufacturing, utilities and resources (mining, oil and gas).
