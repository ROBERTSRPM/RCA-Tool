# Outbound playbook (Roberts, 45 minutes a day)

Offer: 90-day founding-site RCA pilot, 5 sites. Buyers: maintenance, reliability, plant and operations managers at Australian food and beverage sites, 50 to 500 staff. Contact: roberts@intelbeyond.com. Costs are AUD a month and are estimates. Check current pricing.

## 1. Lead sourcing

**Sales Navigator (A$130 to A$170)**
- [ ] Geography: Australia. Headcount: 51-200 and 201-500. Seniority: Manager, Director, Owner.
- [ ] Industry: Food and Beverage Manufacturing, Dairy Product Manufacturing, Baked Goods Manufacturing, Meat Products Manufacturing, Beverage Manufacturing, Sweet and Snack Food Manufacturing.
- [ ] Optional: "Posted on LinkedIn in the last 30 days", "Changed jobs in the last 90 days".
- [ ] Title string:
  `("maintenance manager" OR "reliability manager" OR "reliability engineer" OR "plant manager" OR "factory manager" OR "operations manager" OR "site manager" OR "head of maintenance") NOT (assistant OR intern OR graduate OR apprentice OR sales OR marketing OR recruiter)`
- [ ] Save as a lead list. There is no native export, and scraping extensions breach LinkedIn's terms.

**Apollo.io (no-cost tier, or A$75 to A$100 paid)**
- [ ] People search with the same titles. Location: Australia. Employees: 51-100, 101-200, 201-500.
- [ ] Industry: Food & Beverages, Food Production, Dairy. Email status: Verified.
- [ ] Save to a list, then Export CSV. Exports use credits and the no-cost tier caps them.

**Verification (before any send)**
- [ ] MillionVerifier, Bouncer or NeverBounce (about A$20 to A$60 per 10,000 emails).
- [ ] Send valid only. Drop invalid, disposable and role addresses. Re-verify lists older than 7 days. Bounces under 2%.

**Cheap backups**
- [ ] AFGC members, sector bodies (AMIC, Dairy Australia, Bakers Australia), state food industry associations.
- [ ] Trade directories and exhibitor lists (Foodpro, Fine Food Australia, ICN Gateway).
- [ ] LinkedIn company "People" tab filtered by title, by hand.
- [ ] Log the source URL and date for every contact.

**VA option (Upwork or OnlineJobs)**
Estimate: A$6 to A$12 an hour on OnlineJobs, A$8 to A$23 on Upwork. 5 hours a week is about A$30 to A$115.

Job post brief:
> Build a verified prospect list for an Australian B2B manufacturing service. Find maintenance, reliability, plant and operations managers at Australian food and beverage plants with 50 to 500 staff, using the filters I supply in Sales Navigator and Apollo. Output a Google Sheet: first name, last name, title, company, site, state, website, LinkedIn URL, email, source URL, date. No scraping tools, no bought lists. Paid test: 50 contacts, 90% title match. 5 hours a week.

## 2. Deliverability for 3 inboxes

- [ ] Never send cold mail from intelbeyond.com.
- [ ] Domains: {domain_1}, {domain_2}, {domain_3}, each redirecting to intelbeyond.com.
- [ ] Licensed Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 inboxes (about A$10 each, domains A$20 to A$30 a year). No reseller inboxes or aliases.
- [ ] SPF, DKIM, DMARC on every domain. DMARC starts at p=none with reporting.
- [ ] Warm up 2 to 3 weeks before cold sends.
- [ ] Ramp per inbox: 10 a day, then 15, 20, and 30 by week 6. Follow-ups count.
- [ ] Open and click tracking off. Plain text. No link in email 1.
- [ ] Pause if bounces pass 2% or replies stay under 1% after 300 sends.

**Sending tools:** Instantly A$55 to A$100, Smartlead A$60 to A$130, lemlist A$85 to A$120 per user (estimates).

## 3. Spam Act 2003 (plain terms)

- [ ] Inferred consent needs a conspicuously published work address with no "no unsolicited messages" notice, and a message relevant to the person's role.
- [ ] No address-harvesting software or harvested lists.
- [ ] Identify the sender: name, Intelbeyond, accurate contact details.
- [ ] Working unsubscribe, honoured within 5 working days. Suppress across email and LinkedIn.
- [ ] Keep source URL and date as evidence.
- [ ] Check with a lawyer before sending, especially for addresses from a database rather than a published page.

## 4. Email sequence (60 to 90 words, signature excluded)

Footer on every email: "If this isn't relevant, reply 'no' and I'll stop. Roberts, Intelbeyond, {postal_address}." Roberts confirms every proof line is true.

**Email 1, day 1. Subjects: "repeat stops at {site}" / "same fault, {company}"**
> Hi {first_name},
>
> {personal_line}
>
> Every plant I ran had 1 or 2 faults that kept coming back. The RCA got done once, the actions drifted, and nobody could show whether the countermeasure held.
>
> I ran plants at Veolia, Nippon Sanso, Mars Wrigley and Nestlé. I'm now lining up 5 founding sites for a 90-day RCA pilot.
>
> Is a repeat fault at {site} on your list this quarter?

**Email 2, day 5, same thread. Subjects: "countermeasures that held" / "the 90 days at {site}"**
> Hi {first_name},
>
> More detail on the pilot. We take 1 repeat failure at {site}, run the RCA with your team, track each countermeasure to done, and check whether the failure returned. You finish with a written record your plant manager and auditors can read.
>
> Only 5 founding sites are in. Would a 1-page outline be useful?

**Email 3, day 11. Subjects: "closing this out" / "reliability at {company}"**
> Hi {first_name},
>
> I'll stop here so I don't fill your inbox. If repeat failures at {site} aren't a priority now, that's fine.
>
> If they are, I can send how a founding site runs the 90 days, including your team's weekly time. If reliability sits with someone else at {company}, a name would help.

## 5. LinkedIn sequence

- [ ] Day 1: view profile, comment on a recent post.
- [ ] Day 2, connection note (under 200 characters, write it yourself):
> Hi {first_name}, ex-plant manager (Mars Wrigley, Nestlé) now working on repeat-failure RCA for Australian food sites. {personal_line} Keen to connect.
- [ ] Day 3 after accept:
> Thanks for connecting, {first_name}. I'm talking with maintenance and reliability people about repeat failures that survive the first RCA. How does {site} check that a countermeasure held? Happy to swap notes.
- [ ] Day 10 after accept:
> {first_name}, last note from me. I'm lining up 5 founding sites for a 90-day RCA pilot. Want a 1-page outline? No worries if not.
- [ ] Stop after 3 messages. A reply on any channel stops all channels.

## 6. Daily routine (45 minutes)

- [ ] 10 min: list building and verification.
- [ ] 15 min: write 10 {personal_line} entries from the site's website, news or a recent post. Real facts with a source. No hook found means send without one.
- [ ] 10 min: LinkedIn, 5 requests or comments.
- [ ] 10 min: replies. Positive replies get 2 proposed times the same day.
